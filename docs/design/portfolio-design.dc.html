03 — About

In the summer of 2019 I found out how much fun it is to code: an idea in the morning, a working program by evening. I haven't stopped since.

I came to software the long way round. I worked with automotive diagnostics and customers at Volvo Cars, with manufacturing and continuous improvement at Scania, and spent five years living and working in the UK and France. Fixing cars and production lines turns out to be good training for debugging.

To me, technology is half the job. The other half — working through a problem with a team, or explaining something clearly to the people who use it — is at least as interesting, and it's where I do my best work.

In code, I care about how easy it is to change: clear structure, no more moving parts than needed, and tooling that helps the next person.
